Part 2: Reading Comprehension

Meet the Johnson family. Robert is the father, and he is a doctor. He works in a big hospital. In his free time, he likes playing tennis. Susan is the mother, and she is an accountant. She works in an office. She loves reading books. Robert and Susan have two children, Emily and Jack. Emily is fourteen years old, and she is a student. She studies geography and literature. Her hobby is swimming. Jack is eleven years old, and he is also a student. His favorite subject is mathematics. On Saturdays, the whole family eats lunch together in the garden because they love spending time together.
